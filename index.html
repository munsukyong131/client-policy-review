Client Policy Review

Upload a client's policy detail PDFs or screenshots and get a clear policy summary, with points to discuss and internal advisor notes.

Open the app

Opens on claude.ai. Sign in with your Claude account; no API key needed.

What you get

Client details & at-a-glance view
Policy overview
Coverage by benefit
Investment funds
Nomination & ownership
Points to discuss with the client
Advisor notes (internal)
Client copy without the notes

How to use it

  1. Press Open the app and sign in to claude.ai.
  2. Upload all of one client's policy detail PDFs or screenshots.
  3. Press Write policy summary. The first time, allow the page to use Claude.
  4. Download the client copy, or the version with advisor notes for your file.

Privacy

  • Files are read in your browser and sent to Claude only when you press the button.
  • IC, email, phone and card numbers in PDF text are hidden before sending (on by default). Screenshots are sent as they are.
  • Figures are copied from the records and benefits are never guessed from plan names. Always check the summary against the policy contract before giving it to a client.
