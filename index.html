Client Policy Review

Upload a client's policy detail PDFs or screenshots and get a clear policy summary, with points to discuss and internal advisor notes.

SETUP

Your Anthropic API key

Needed to write summaries. Create one at console.anthropic.com under API keys; use is billed to your Anthropic account. The key is saved only in this browser on this device and is sent only to Anthropic. Set a monthly spend limit in the console, and don't save the key on shared computers.

Policy summary

The client's summary appears here

Upload the policy detail pages for one client (one PDF or screenshot per policy is fine) and press Write policy summary. You get:

  • Client details and an at-a-glance view: policies, premium payable, fund value
  • Policy overview and coverage by benefit across all policies
  • Investment funds, nomination and ownership
  • Points to discuss with the client
  • Advisor notes (internal): inconsistencies and things to verify

Figures are copied exactly from the records. Benefits are never guessed from plan names; anything inferred is marked and kept in the advisor notes.

Files are read in this browser and sent to Anthropic with your API key only when you press the button. The client copy leaves out the advisor notes. Check the summary against the policy contract before giving it to the client.
